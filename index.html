How much solar subsidy can I get in Tamil Nadu?

Eligible households in Tamil Nadu can receive up to ₹1,00,000 in combined government subsidies—up to ₹78,000 from Central PM Surya Ghar and up to ₹22,000 from Tamil Nadu State rooftop solar subsidy.

Can solar panels reduce my electricity bill to zero?

Yes. When accurately sized to match your bi-monthly TANGEDCO or monthly KSEB usage, daytime units exported to the grid offset consumption charges via net-metering, leaving only minimal statutory fixed charges.

What is the difference between On-Grid, Off-Grid, and Hybrid systems?

On-Grid connects directly to TANGEDCO/KSEB for net-metering and maximum subsidies. Off-Grid stores power in batteries for remote areas. Hybrid (BESS) delivers seamless blackout backup plus grid bill savings.
