How much solar subsidy can I get in Tamil Nadu?

Eligible households in Tamil Nadu can receive up to ₹1,00,000 in combined government subsidies—comprising up to ₹78,000 from the Central Government (PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana) and up to ₹22,000 from the Tamil Nadu State additional rooftop solar subsidy.

Can solar panels reduce my electricity bill to zero?

Yes. When your solar system is accurately sized to match your bi-monthly TANGEDCO or monthly KSEB consumption, the daytime units exported to the grid offset your consumption charges via net-metering, leaving only minimal statutory fixed charges.

What is the difference between On-Grid, Off-Grid, and Hybrid systems?

On-Grid connects directly to the utility grid for net-metering and qualifies for maximum subsidies. Off-Grid uses batteries for areas without grid reliability. Hybrid (BESS) combines both—delivering seamless blackout backup alongside grid bill savings.
