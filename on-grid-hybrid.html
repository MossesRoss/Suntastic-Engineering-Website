On-Grid Solar System

Grid-Tied Bi-Directional Net-Metering

Synchronizes directly with TANGEDCO or KSEB utility lines without batteries. Surplus daytime solar energy is exported to the grid and credited against your night-time consumption.

  • Eligible for up to ₹1,00,000 PM Surya Ghar + TN State subsidy
  • Fastest payback period (3 to 4 years) with zero battery replacement cost
  • Reduces bi-monthly electricity bills by up to 90%–100%

Zero-Downtime + Grid Export 02

Hybrid Solar (BESS)

Net-Metering + Lithium-LFP Storage

Combines utility net-metering with intelligent Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery banks. Powers critical loads seamlessly during power cuts while exporting excess units once batteries are full.

  • Millisecond switchover (< 10ms) for computers, medical gear & ACs
  • Exports excess solar power via TANGEDCO/KSEB bidirectional meter
  • 10-year Lithium-LFP battery life with 6,000+ charge cycles
Request Hybrid BESS Quote
Remote & Standalone 03

Off-Grid Solar System

100% Grid-Independent Power

Designed for farmhouses, estates, hill stations, and rural facilities facing frequent voltage fluctuations or no utility grid access. Operates independently on solar and battery storage.

  • No TANGEDCO/KSEB export approval required
  • High-surge MPPT inverters built for water pumps & heavy inductive loads
  • Compatible with Tubular C10 or Lithium-LFP battery banks
