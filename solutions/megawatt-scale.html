Enterprise engineering scope for Megawatt assets.

Every Megawatt project is engineered in-house to maximize Capacity Utilization Factor (CUF), eliminate balance-of-system bottlenecks, and pass rigorous lender and utility inspections.

Resource & Yield Engineering

Geotechnical soil testing, contour & hydrological drainage mapping, P50/P90 PVsyst energy yield simulations, and shadow-optimized pitch & DC/AC overloading design.

Civil & Structural Foundations

STAAD.Pro wind-load certified hot-dip GI / Galvalume fixed-tilt or single-axis tracker structures, cast-in-situ pile foundations, perimeter fencing, and internal roads.

Tier-1 PV & Inverter Stations

ALMM-compliant N-Type TOPCon / Mono-PERC bifacial modules paired with high-efficiency 1500V DC string or central inverters and inverter duty transformers (IDTs).

HT Evacuation & Pooling

11 kV / 33 kV / 66 kV switchgear rooms, overhead transmission lines or XLPE underground cabling, metering bays, and complete CEIG & DISCOM statutory clearances.

SCADA & Weather Telemetry

IEC 61850 / Modbus plant SCADA control rooms with Class-A pyranometers, module temperature sensors, PPC (Power Plant Controller), and real-time SLDC data integration.

Testing, Handover & O&M

IV-curve tracing, thermography audits, PR acceptance testing, comprehensive as-built handover dossiers, and long-term preventive O&M with plant availability SLAs.
