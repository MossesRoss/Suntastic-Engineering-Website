Substation Topologies

Engineered for urban compactness or open-yard scalability.

66/11 kV & 33/11 kV Indoor GIS

Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS)

Hermetically sealed SF6 metal-enclosed switchgear bays requiring up to 70% less land than conventional switchyards—immune to coastal salt fog, industrial dust, and humidity.

End-to-End Substation Execution

Design, civil, switchgear, protection, and charging.

System & Protection Engineering

Short-circuit & load-flow analysis, IEEE 80 earth-mat grid design, insulation coordination, relay protection settings, and utility-approved Single Line Diagrams (SLDs).

Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS)

Supply, erection, SF6 gas handling, and high-voltage testing of 33 kV and 66 kV indoor GIS feeder, transformer, and bus-coupler bays.

Air-Insulated Switchgear (AIS)

Outdoor switchyard steel gantries, SF6 / vacuum circuit breakers, motorized isolators, instrument transformers (CT/PT), surge arrestors, and busbar stringing.

Power Transformers & CRP

EHV/HV power transformers with On-Load Tap Changers (OLTC), RTCC panels, Control & Relay Panels (CRP), and redundant 110V/220V DC battery-charger systems.

Civil, Cable Trenches & Fire Systems

GIS control buildings, EOT cranes, transformer foundations with baffle walls & oil-soak pits, RCC cable trenches, and nitrogen injection / HVW spray fire protection.

Pre-Commissioning & CEIG Charging

Primary & secondary relay injection, SF6 dew-point/leakage verification, transformer oil BDV & tan-delta testing, CEIG statutory inspection, and grid synchronization.

Engineering Selection Guide

GIS vs. AIS Substation Architecture

Parameter Gas-Insulated Substation (GIS) Air-Insulated Substation (AIS)
Land / Footprint Requirement Up to 70% smaller footprint (Indoor modular bays) Larger open switchyard area required for air clearances
Environmental & Coastal Immunity Completely sealed against salt spray, pollution & wildlife Exposed insulators require periodic washing in coastal/dusty zones
Maintenance & Reliability Minimal maintenance; 10+ year major inspection intervals Routine insulator, isolator, and breaker mechanism servicing
Standards & Automation IEC 62271-203, IEC 61850 SAS, Online SF6 density monitoring IEC 62271, CBIP Substation Manual, IEC 61850 CRP
Best Suited For Urban utilities, industrial estates, coastal & space-constrained sites Solar pooling substations, rural grid yards & bay extensions
