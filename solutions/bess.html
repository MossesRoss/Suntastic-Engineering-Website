Dispatchable Energy Architecture

Three intelligent operating modes for utilities and industries.

Solar Smoothing & Time-Shifting

Absorbing Surplus Solar Generation

During peak midday irradiance, the BESS absorbs excess solar MW to eliminate curtailment, smooth cloud-induced ramp rates, and store clean energy for evening dispatch.

Turnkey BESS Integration

Engineered for safety, thermal endurance, and grid compliance.

Liquid-Cooled LFP Enclosures

20ft / 40ft IP55 containerized Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery racks with precision liquid thermal management keeping cell-to-cell ΔT < 3°C even in 48°C Indian summers.

Bi-Directional PCS & Step-Up

1500V DC 4-quadrant Power Conversion Systems supporting active/reactive power regulation, synthetic inertia, black-start capability, and MV step-up transformers.

Multi-Tier BMS & Local EMS

3-level Battery Management System (Cell, Rack, Container) integrated with an on-site Energy Management System for automated peak-shaving, ToD arbitrage, and ramp control.

UL 9540A Fire & Gas Protection

Multi-stage early off-gas (H2/CO) & thermal runaway detection, explosion venting panels, and automated aerosol / water-mist suppression engineered per NFPA 855.

Grid Synchronization & SCADA

IEC 61850 / DNP3 / Modbus TCP control interfaces for seamless integration with existing solar plants, substations, and SLDC / RLDC utility dispatch centers.

Testing, Commissioning & Handover

Civil plinths, AC/DC cabling, earthing, Factory & Site Acceptance Testing (FAT/SAT), round-trip efficiency validation, CEIG approvals, and turnkey handover.
