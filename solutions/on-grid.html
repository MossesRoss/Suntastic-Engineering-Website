How Bi-Directional Net-Metering Works

Use solar by day, bank credits with the utility grid.

Daytime Surplus Export

Powering Loads & Banking Units

Solar DC is converted to grid-synchronized AC. Your home or factory consumes solar first, and every excess kWh automatically exports through the bi-directional meter as energy credit.

System Architecture & BOM

Engineered for high uptime and regulatory compliance.

ALMM / DCR Modules

IP65 Grid-Tied Inverters

98%+ conversion efficiency, multi-MPPT tracking, zero-export readiness, and IEC 62116 anti-islanding.

ACDB / DCDB Protection

Type-II DC & AC surge protection devices, UV-resistant solar DC cabling, and dedicated copper earthing.

End-to-End Approvals
