How much solar subsidy can you get in Tamil Nadu?

Central and state support for rooftop solar at a glance.

Read the guide

Eligible households in Tamil Nadu can receive up to ₹1,00,000 in combined subsidy under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana and the Tamil Nadu Government’s additional rooftop solar subsidy.

  • Central subsidy: up to ₹78,000
  • Tamil Nadu state subsidy: up to ₹22,000
  • Total: up to ₹1,00,000

The amount depends on system capacity and eligibility. The state subsidy applies to eligible PM Surya Ghar registrations made on or after 5 August 2026, subject to scheme guidelines, approved installation, commissioning, and net metering. Subsidies are paid after verification and approval, and cover residential consumers only. Always check the current guidelines on the official portal.

On-grid, off-grid or hybrid: which solar system is right for you?

What each system does and who it suits.

Read the guide

An on-grid system connects to the electricity grid and reduces the power you buy. An off-grid system uses batteries to supply power without depending on the grid. A hybrid system combines grid-connected solar with battery storage.

The best option depends on your budget, backup needs, electricity connection, and local regulations. A site visit and a review of your recent bills will give a far better answer than a rule of thumb.

Residential 2 min read

What size solar system does your home need?

An indicative guide from monthly units to system size.

Read the guide

System size depends on your monthly consumption, available rooftop area, daytime usage, and budget. As a rough guide:

  • 200–300 units a month: about 3 kW, around 175 sq ft of roof
  • 300–500 units: about 5 kW, around 285 sq ft
  • 500–700 units: 6–8 kW, around 330–450 sq ft
  • Above 700 units: about 10 kW, around 570 sq ft

These are indicative figures. A site inspection and your actual bills give a more reliable estimate than household size alone.

Can solar panels bring your electricity bill to zero?

Realistic expectations on savings.

Read the guide

Solar can significantly reduce your bill by generating power for your own use. Actual savings depend on your usage, system capacity, sunlight, tariff structure, and net-metering rules. Fixed charges and other fees may still apply, so a zero bill cannot be guaranteed for every property.

Commercial and industrial rooftop solar: what to evaluate

Questions to settle before you commit.

Read the guide

Commercial and industrial systems are designed around electricity demand, available space, operating schedule, and project economics. Ask for a feasibility assessment with projected savings based on your actual consumption data.

Accelerated tax depreciation benefits can apply to commercial and industrial owners. Confirm the current rules with your tax advisor.

Before you choose a solar company: what to check

Warranties, empanelment, and after-sales support.

Read the guide
  • Ask for the specific warranties: panel performance, inverter, workmanship, and any exclusions.
  • Verify the company’s current empanelment on the official scheme portal before ordering.
  • Confirm maintenance coverage, response times, and warranty terms in the quotation and service agreement.
  • Check that your installation and application meet the subsidy requirements.
